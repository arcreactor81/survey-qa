Coverage and verdict are two axes and are never merged into one badge.   • ≡Ambiguous — withheld AMBIGUOUS — The document admits two or more typed readings at this locus, so site judgment is withheld. Withheld rows are visible but are counted as neither pass nor fail. Counts as: withheld.

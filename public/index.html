Survey QA — Language Check PoC

Automated language QA for programmed surveys. A headless browser walks every page of the survey under test, then two LLMs (Claude and DeepSeek) compare the rendered text against the questionnaire document (.docx) and report discrepancies — typos, missing options, broken piping, mislabeled scales, and more — with verified quotes and a seeded-error scorecard.

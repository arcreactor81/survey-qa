Automated questionnaire-to-website verification

Survey QA

Survey QA automates the link-testing step of market-research fieldwork. Point it at the Word questionnaire and the programmed survey URL: it parses the .docx into a canonical spec, walks every survey page with a real browser, has three models from three families — DeepSeek v4-pro, Grok 4.3 (xAI) and Claude Sonnet 4.6 (Anthropic) — independently compare the rendered survey against the spec, combines their findings by consensus and verifies each with verbatim quotes, and delivers a findings report with a seeded-error scorecard. Typos, missing options, broken piping, mislabeled scales — caught before a single respondent sees them.

Pipeline

How it works

1

Questionnaire parsed

The Word document (.docx) is parsed into a spec: questions, options, scales, piping and programmer notes.

2

Browser walks the pages

A real headless browser steps through every page of the live survey, capturing text, screenshots and PDFs.

3

Three-model comparison

DeepSeek v4-pro, Grok 4.3 and Claude Sonnet 4.6 independently compare every rendered page against the spec, all routed through the Cloudflare AI Gateway.

4

Quote verification

Every finding must cite verbatim quotes from spec and site — each quote is mechanically checked before it counts.

5

Scored report

A findings report with a seeded-error scorecard: recall per model, false positives, cost and latency.
